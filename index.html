How It Works

01

Connect Wallet

Use Phantom to authenticate. No passwords, no email — just your keys.

02

Register Device

Add your solar panel, wind turbine or battery to the protocol.

03

Verify & Mint

Oracle verifies energy output. You mint SRC tokens — 1 SRC = 1 MWh.

04

Trade or Hold

SRC tokens are transferable, tradeable and backed by real energy data.

SRC Token

Solar Renewable Certificate. Every token represents 1 MWh of verified energy production. Max supply: 1,000,000,000 SRC. Built on Solana for near-zero fees and instant finality.
